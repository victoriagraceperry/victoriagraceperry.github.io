ABOUT

A hands-on engineer.

I’m an Electrical Engineering student at Yale University, with a second major in Comparative Literature. I like projects where the boundary between disciplines disappears: a motor becomes a control problem, a sensor becomes a signal-processing problem, and a research question becomes something you can measure and build.

Outside the classroom, I work on Yale’s Formula SAE electric race car through Bulldogs Racing and have experience designing electromechanical systems, writing control software, machining parts, building robots, and analyzing experimental data.
